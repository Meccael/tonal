# Notice and third-party material

Tonal is an unofficial, non-commercial fork of **Minima Player by BKR APPS**.
It is not affiliated with or endorsed by BKR APPS.

## Inherited from Minima Player (not covered by the MIT licence)

Tonal began as a revival of Minima Player's decompiled sources. Most of the
code has since been rewritten (see the README), but some parts are still
derived from the original and remain the work of BKR APPS:

- The overall visual design and interface concept.
- Remaining inherited classes, including the Home playlists fragment and the
  audio-regulation (equalizer) fragment, and some class names.
- The English strings and the community translations that are keyed to them.

No licence was published with Minima Player, and its author could not be
reached. If you are the author or a rights holder and want any of this
removed or changed, open an issue or contact the maintainer and it will be
dealt with promptly.
